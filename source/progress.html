This review confirms real integrated movement in the last cycle: primary now has generated-C $GLOBALS[...] write/unset/append paths, request-superglobal root/nested appends, request-superglobal null-coalescing reads, and direct unresolved root-variable reads routed through shared runtime/compiler ABIs.
